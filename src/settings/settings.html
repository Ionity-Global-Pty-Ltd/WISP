Aura Wisp — a living light that follows your focus

Your focus, glowing.

WISP wraps the window you are working in with a soft aura, lights the edges of the active screen, and pulls the light across whenever you switch. Everything here applies live.

  • Ctrl+Alt+W pause / resume
  • Ctrl+Alt+Shift+W next colour
  • Tray icon → every setting, anytime
